About PosterPal

PosterPal is a free poster and caption maker for small businesses. Salons, shops, restaurants, boutiques, churches and event organisers can make a clean, professional poster in about a minute, with no design skills, no sign-up and no app to install.

Most small businesses need to post new offers every day on Facebook, Instagram and WhatsApp Status, but few have the time or the tools to design them. PosterPal gives you ready-made designs, so you only type your details and download.

How it works

  1. Choose your business type: salon, shop, restaurant, boutique, or church and events.
  2. Pick one of nine poster designs and an accent colour.
  3. Type your business name, headline, price or offer, details and phone number.
  4. Download your poster as a PNG image and copy the caption that is written for you, with emojis and hashtags.

Questions

Is PosterPal really free? Yes. There are no download limits, no watermark and no account to create.

Where can I use my posters? The square image works well on Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp Status and Telegram, and it prints well on a flyer.

Is my information saved? No. Your details stay in your browser. The poster is made on your own device and is never uploaded.
